Hasan Amin

I am a mathematics graduate currently pursuing the International Mathematics Master (IMM) programme at Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS) in Pakistan.

I completed my undergraduate degree in Mathematics at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) in Islamabad, Pakistan.

My mathematical interests include differential geometry, topology, Lie groups, and mathematical physics, particularly their connections with geometry and the study of symmetry.

My undergraduate final year project was a review of the Poincaré Conjecture and Perelman's proof, focusing on the role of Ricci flow.

I have also studied the Hopf fibration and spectral sequences, including their connections with the topology of spheres and the geometry of \(SU(2)\).

You can find some of my code on GitHub.

A full academic history can be made available in my CV.
