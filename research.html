Research Interests

My research interests include Differential Geometry, Lie theory and mathematical physics., and I am eager to explore new challenges and opportunities in these fields.

Selected Projects and Academic Work

Octonionic Projective Spaces

This project is my masters thesis and focuses on the construction of the octonionic projective space. This is done by constructing the exceptional Lie group \(G_2\) as the automorphism group of the octonions, then construct the Lie Group \(F_4\) as a subgroup of \(Spin(9)\) to construct \(\mathbb{OP}^2\). We also aim to study higher dimensional projective spaces.

The Poincaré Conjecture and Perelman's Proof

An undergraduate final-year project reviewing the Poincaré Conjecture and Perelman's proof, with emphasis on Ricci flow and its role in three-dimensional geometry and topology.

The Hopf Fibration and Spectral Sequences

A study of the Hopf fibration \(S^1 \to S^3 \to S^2\) and the use of spectral sequences to understand the topology of spheres and the geometry of \(SU(2)\).
